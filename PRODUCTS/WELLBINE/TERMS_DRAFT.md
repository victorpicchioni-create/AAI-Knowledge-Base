# What Wellbine Is

Wellbine is an adaptive wellness guidance system designed to help users organize daily routines, wellness behaviors and habit execution across areas such as sleep, movement, hydration, nutrition, mind, sunlight, recovery and Daily Stack.

Wellbine may use user-provided information, app activity, optional wearable data, optional uploads and AI-assisted systems to personalize the user experience.

Wellbine is designed to support wellness routines.

Wellbine is not designed to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease.

---

# Wellness Guidance Boundary

Wellbine provides wellness guidance, organization tools, behavioral prompts, adaptive routines and contextual recommendations.

Wellbine does not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, emergency care or professional medical advice.

Any guidance provided by Wellbine should be understood as general wellness support.

Users are responsible for deciding whether guidance is appropriate for their own circumstances.

---

# No Medical Advice

Wellbine does not replace doctors, licensed healthcare professionals, therapists, nutritionists, pharmacists or other qualified professionals.

Users should consult qualified professionals before:

- Starting a new medical treatment
- Stopping a medical treatment
- Changing medication
- Changing dosage
- Ignoring symptoms
- Making major diet changes
- Making major exercise changes
- Acting on health-related concerns
- Using supplements in connection with medical conditions

Wellbine should not be used as a substitute for professional judgment.

---

# No Emergency Use

Wellbine is not an emergency service.

Users should not use Wellbine for urgent or emergency situations.

In an emergency, users should contact local emergency services or seek immediate medical assistance.

Wellbine does not monitor users in real time for emergencies.

Wellbine does not guarantee detection of health problems, risks or urgent events.

Wellbine may use AI-assisted systems to help personalize guidance, organize user context, generate daily suggestions, interpret app activity, summarize uploads, support recommendations and improve the user experience.
