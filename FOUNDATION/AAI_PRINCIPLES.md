# AAI Principles

**Version:** 0.1.0

**Status:** Active

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the permanent principles that guide the Adaptive Alignment Intelligence (AAI) framework.

These principles are independent of products, technologies and implementations.

Every framework, product or feature built on AAI must respect these principles.

---

# Principle 1

## Context Before Action

AAI must understand context before generating recommendations, interventions or system behavior.

Context is the starting point of intelligence.

---

# Principle 2

## Learning Never Stops

AAI-based systems must improve through interaction, feedback, patterns and outcomes.

Every interaction should increase future usefulness.

---

# Principle 3

## Anticipation Before Reaction

AAI should not only respond to explicit requests.

Whenever possible, it should anticipate needs before they become obvious to the user or system.

---

# Principle 4

## Alignment Is Continuous

Alignment is not a one-time correction.

It is a continuous process of keeping a system connected to its objective as context changes.

---

# Principle 5

## Outcomes Matter

AAI exists to improve outcomes.

Information, recommendations and interventions should only exist when they help produce better results.

---

# Principle 6

## Intelligence Should Reduce Complexity

AAI must reduce cognitive load.

A system powered by AAI should make the next step clearer, not harder.

---

# Principle 7

## The Best Intelligence Feels Natural

Users should benefit from intelligence without needing to understand the internal system.

The experience should feel simple, timely and useful.

---

# Principle 8

## Interventions Must Have Purpose

AAI should not interrupt, notify or guide without a clear reason.

Every intervention must have a meaningful role in improving alignment or outcomes.

---

# Principle 9

## Technology Is Not The Framework

AAI is independent of any specific AI model, LLM, provider, app builder, backend or software stack.

Technology is implementation.

AAI is architecture.

---

# Principle 10

## Products May Change, The Framework Remains

Interfaces, features and products may evolve.

The foundational principles of AAI should remain stable.
