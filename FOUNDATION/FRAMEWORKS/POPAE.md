**Version:** 0.1.0

**Status:** Active

**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines POPAE, the core operating cycle of Adaptive Alignment Intelligence (AAI).

POPAE describes how AAI-based systems prepare context, observe signals, predict needs, align actions and evolve over time.

---

# Official Definition

**POPAE is the adaptive operating cycle of AAI, composed of five stages: Prepare, Observe, Predict, Align and Evolve.**

---

# Core Structure

POPAE is composed of five sequential and continuous stages:

```text
Prepare

↓

Observe

↓

Predict

↓

Align

↓

Evolve
```

This cycle repeats continuously.

Each cycle improves the system's ability to understand context, anticipate needs and optimize outcomes.

---

# Stage 1: Prepare

Prepare is the stage where the system organizes the environment before making recommendations or interventions.

The objective is to reduce noise and establish useful context.

Examples:

- Clean unnecessary inputs.
- Identify relevant user state.
- Remove irrelevant signals.
- Prepare the system for interpretation.
- Establish the current operating context.

In AAI, preparation comes before observation because poor context produces poor intelligence.

---

# Stage 2: Observe

Observe is the stage where the system collects relevant signals.

Observation may include:

- User behavior
- User feedback
- System state
- Environmental inputs
- Historical patterns
- Preferences
- Biological or operational signals
- External conditions

Observation should be selective.

AAI should not collect signals without purpose.

---

# Stage 3: Predict

Predict is the stage where the system anticipates likely needs, risks, opportunities or changes.

Prediction may include:

- What the user may need next.
- What context is emerging.
- What action may produce better alignment.
- What risk may appear if no intervention occurs.
- What outcome is likely based on current patterns.

Prediction is what allows AAI to move beyond reaction.

---

# Stage 4: Align

Align is the stage where the system generates the most appropriate intervention, recommendation or adjustment.

Alignment should always be:

- Context-aware
- Useful
- Timely
- Simple
- Actionable
- Low-friction

The goal is not to control the user or system.

The goal is to restore or improve alignment toward the desired outcome.

---

# Stage 5: Evolve

Evolve is the stage where the system learns from what happened.

The system evaluates:

- Was the recommendation useful?
- Did the user respond?
- Was the outcome improved?
- Did the context change?
- Should future guidance change?

Evolution allows AAI-based systems to become more useful over time.

---

# Operating Logic

POPAE follows this logic:

```text
Prepare the context.

Observe relevant signals.

Predict what may happen next.

Align the system through useful intervention.

Evolve based on feedback and outcome.
```

# Relationship With AAI

AAI is the Deep Intelligence Architecture.

POPAE is the operating cycle that defines how AAI behaves.

In simple terms:

**AAI is the architecture.**

**POPAE is the cycle.**

# Product Independence

POPAE is not specific to Wellbine.

It can be applied to any product or system built using AAI.

Examples of possible domains:

- Health
- Performance
- Education
- Productivity
- Operations
- Finance
- Customer experience
- Personal guidance systems

Each product may implement POPAE differently, but the cycle remains the same.

---

# Implementation Rules

Any POPAE-based system should follow these rules:

- Prepare before observing.
- Observe before predicting.
- Predict before intervening.
- Align without increasing unnecessary complexity.
- Learn from every cycle.
- Avoid interventions without clear purpose.
- Prioritize context over generic automation.

---

# What POPAE Is Not

POPAE is not a user interface.

POPAE is not a checklist.

POPAE is not a fixed workflow.

POPAE is not a chatbot flow.

POPAE is not limited to health or wellness.

POPAE is the adaptive operating cycle of AAI.

---

It serves as the foundation for product-specific frameworks such as Wellbine's biological context methodology.

These additions should extend POPAE without changing its five core stages.
